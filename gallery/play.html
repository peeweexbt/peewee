how it works: peewee does his own thing — wanders, sits in the sun, grooms, naps in his bed. tap him to say hi and open the toy box. the feather follows your finger; tap the floor to toss the yarn; the mouse runs on its own and he'll stalk it; the fish is a snack that brings his energy back. when he's tired he'll put himself to bed. be patient with him, he's a small cat.
